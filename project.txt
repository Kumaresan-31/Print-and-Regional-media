1. EXECUTIVE PROJECT OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Automated ePaper Harvester (VEE2) is an enterprise-grade, scheduled newspaper
harvesting and regional media intelligence platform. It automates the end-to-end
lifecycle of print and digital newspapers across India, supporting 57+ configured
publications in 10+ languages (English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Marathi, Bengali,
Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, Punjabi, and Urdu).

Key capabilities include:
- Scheduled early-morning broadsheet harvesting with anti-bot mitigation.
- Active session cookie injection for paywalled editions (The Hindu, Lokmat,
  Loksatta, DT Next, Financial Express, Times of India).
- Multi-tier Optical Character Recognition (OCR) combining RapidOCR (ONNX),
  Tesseract OCR, and PaddleOCR for accurate regional text recognition.
- Dual-channel reading experience: Online News (Google News RSS live web feed)
  and ePaper Digital News (harvested broadsheet pages).
- Digital Twin 3-Tier Traceability: Verified English Translation -> Ground-Truth
  OCR -> Scanned Scanned High-Resolution Broadsheet Snapshot.
- Intelligent Inverted Full-Text Search across thousands of indexed newspaper pages
  with automatic clipping extraction and executive PDF report export.
- Multi-channel notification and alert dispatch:
  * Enterprise WhatsApp Bot (Green API integration)
  * Telegram Bot (@Mhjkbktbot) with command polling
  * Inbound IMAP email inbox monitor & outbound SMTP HTML email digests
  * Real-time WebSocket live streaming dashboard and REST API.

   - For non-cookie publications (like Times of India), clicking "ePaper Digital News"
     reveals the "🔔 Subscribe Now ↗" button, taking users to the publication's official
     subscription portal.

   - Full auditability for enterprise compliance and media intelligence:
     * Tier 1: Ground-Truth Raw OCR Text extracted from the physical broadsheet page.
     * Tier 2: Verified English Translation with preserved named entities (e.g. PayU, RBI).
     * Tier 3: Visual Scanned Page Snapshot with highlighted bounding box coordinates.
   - Editorial review workflow to dispute or confirm confidence scores.

   - Web UI (`upload.html`) allows users to upload physical newspaper PDFs.
   - Multi-tier OCR pipeline executes full-page OCR from Page 1 to end.
   - Automatic story segmentation identifies separate article bounding boxes.
   - Translates regional headlines & snippets to English and tags across 19 categories.
   - Generates high-resolution cropped clipping images for each story.

E. Inverted Full-Text Broadsheet Search Engine:
   - High-speed tokenized inverted index built across all harvested broadsheet pages.
   - Real-time search by keyword, company name, entity, date range, or publication.
   - Instant visual rendering of exact clipping crops with highlighted matches.
   - Export single search result or batch results into high-resolution executive PDF reports.

6. QUICK REFERENCE COMMANDS
