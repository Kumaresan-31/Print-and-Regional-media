New education initiative announced across Tamil Nadu

BYLINE: SPECIAL CORRESPONDENT • FILED: 20 SEP 2026, 04:30 IST

The state government today unveiled a comprehensive digital literacy curriculum targeting over 4,000 rural schools. Primary education minister confirmed allocation of ₹240 crore for classroom infrastructure and fiber-optic connectivity hubs, scheduled for roll-out across 14 target districts ahead of the upcoming legislative budget assembly.

According to the administrative directive published in section four, each institution will receive dedicated interactive computational panels and vernacular educational software designed by regional academic consortia. Training workshops for an initial cohort of 12,000 headmasters and mathematics educators will commence late next week.
